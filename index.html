The New Heights of Living at Old Klang Road

Inspired by The Promise of Dawn

Inspired by the magnificent and breathtaking Northern Lights, M AURORA kindles the hope of a new dawn where light, elegance, and positive energy form the very soul of its community.

Located on Old Klang Road, Kuala Lumpur, this freehold serviced residence features two modern 46-storey towers set across 5.2 acres of prime land. Offering ample amenities, exceptional accessibility, top-tier security, and a variety of unit layouts, M AURORA provides a home tailored to diverse needs in a stunning and awe-inspiring modern haven.

M Aurora, the latest freehold serviced apartment, is now open for sale. It marks an exciting new chapter in Old Klang Road’s development. This freehold serviced apartment comprises 1,544 units spread across two 46-storey towers on a 5.24-acre site, making it one of the most significant launches in the area this year. Positioned in a mature yet ever-growing neighborhood, this property redefines city living by focusing on long-term value and connectivity. Every aspect is carefully designed to complement future-ready living experiences with integrated smart space planning.

Unlike leasehold projects with tenure restrictions, M Aurora’s freehold status promises enduring value in an area poised for continuous growth. With Old Klang Road undergoing continuous transformation, owning a home here means being part of a community that’s moving forward.

Developed by Mah Sing Group, one of Malaysia's most established property developers with over 79 completed projects nationwide, M Aurora is more than just a residence. This development is a gateway to urban sophistication and lasting property appreciation.

Good to know

M Aurora, answered

M Aurora is a freehold serviced apartment development on Old Klang Road, Kuala Lumpur. Unlike leasehold projects with tenure restrictions, its freehold status promises enduring value in an area poised for continuous growth.

Five layouts across two towers: Type A (1+1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, 556 sq ft), Type B (2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 737 sq ft), Type C (3 bedrooms with balcony, 2 bathrooms, 858 sq ft), Type D (3 bedrooms with balcony, 3 bathrooms, 916 sq ft) and Type E (4 bedrooms with balcony, 3 bathrooms, 1,019 sq ft), with flexibility to combine rooms and create dual-key spaces. See the floor plans above.

Prices start from RM420,000*. Approved permit pricing ranges from RM492,960 to RM856,560 across Towers A and B; the full permit detail is reproduced in the footer. Per-type price lists are available from the sales team, register below to receive one.

The expected date of completion is September 2030, per the advertising and sales permit.

Each unit comes with 1 to 3 car parks, per the advertising and sales permit.

46 named facilities across the Ground and Level 8 podium plans and the Sky Lounge plan, including a 50m lap pool, gym, yoga studio, co-working space, glamping dome, Aurora Garden and a multi-purpose hall for badminton and pickleball. M Aurora has been awarded the Silver Provisional GreenRE Certification. See the full facilities list.
